## Full description (≤4000 chars)
汉语拼音 (Hanyu Pinyin) helps you master Chinese pinyin — from the building blocks to fast, confident typing.

学习 LEARN
• 23 initials (声母) and all common finals (韵母), each with an example character — tap any card to hear native Mandarin pronunciation
• The four tones (声调) explained with audio examples
• How pinyin input methods work, step by step
• Fast-typing techniques: full-sentence input, 简拼 first-letter shortcuts, and typing ü as v

练习 PRACTICE — 10 interactive drills
• 汉字练习 Characters: see a character, type its pinyin
• 词语练习 Words: type the pinyin of common words
• 声母 / 韵母练习 Initials & finals drills
• 长句练习 Sentences: whole-sentence fast typing, shortcuts accepted
• 简拼速打 Shortcut drill: type only the first letters
• 长信息练习 Long messages for sustained speed
• ü → v 练习: practice the v-for-ü keyboard technique
• 限时挑战 60-second speed challenge
• 综合练习 Mixed review
Every round gives instant feedback with the correct toned pinyin, plus your accuracy and characters-per-minute score.

发音 AUDIO
• Native Mandarin text-to-speech for every card and prompt
• Choose a female (女声) or male (男声) reading voice in Settings
• Works fully offline once a Chinese voice is installed

Perfect for students of Chinese, parents helping children with 汉语, and anyone who wants to type Chinese faster with the pinyin input method.
